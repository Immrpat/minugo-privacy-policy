Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Minugo is an everyday timer app by Namib Co., Ltd. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the Minugo Android app (package name com.minugo.app).

Minugo works offline. It has no account, server connection, advertising, or analytics. Your timer information stays in private storage on your device.

Information Minugo stores

To provide timers, Minugo stores information on your device, such as active timer state, selected activities and durations, favorites, and timer labels you choose. This information is held in the app's private storage. Minugo does not send it to Namib Co., Ltd. or any other party.

Alarms and notifications

When you start a timer, Minugo asks Android to schedule its alarm and, when the timer finishes, show a notification. The app may also restore scheduled timers after a device restart or relevant time change. These functions use Android's built-in alarm and notification services on your device. Notification permission and exact-alarm access are controlled in Android settings.

Timer labels may appear in notifications. You can manage notification visibility, sound, and other alert settings in Android. Minugo does not use location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.

Sharing and third parties

Minugo contains no account service, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or app backend. It does not transmit your timer information. The app's timer data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

Retention and deletion

You can complete or cancel active timers in the app. Saved durations and favorites remain in app storage until you clear Minugo's storage in Android settings or uninstall Minugo. Because Minugo has no accounts or remote database, there is no account deletion process or server copy to request deletion from.

Security

Timer information is stored in Android's app-private storage and is not transmitted by Minugo. Protect your device with Android's available screen-lock and security features. No method of storing information on a device can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children's privacy

Minugo does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require an account and does not transmit information from the device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page shows when the current version took effect. We will update this page when a change is made.
